# Export: "Travel and day log", and the travel log Sheet

One export per tax year: **"Travel and day log – YYYY/YY"** (`Travel and day log YYYY-YY.pdf` and `.csv`; a year in progress adds "(to D Mon YYYY)"). Engine `~/nomad-pro-engine` (run `engine-setup` first if missing).

1. Rebuild `daylog.json` from the Sheet (below). If the year has unlogged days, run a catch-up and ask once whether to fill them first. Gaps stay visible either way.
2. From `~/nomad-pro-data`: `python3 ~/nomad-pro-engine/tools/render_pdf.py daylog.json --tax-year YYYY/YY --out-dir exports/ --data-root . --as-of <today> --kb hmrc/pages --html-only` (writes `.html` and `.csv`). Add the Source footer (below) to both, make the PDF from the HTML with WeasyPrint (`HTML(string=html, base_url=None).write_pdf(...)`) and delete the `.html`. Row IDs are `D-` plus the date, so every CSV row and PDF day maps to a `Days` row.
3. Run `~/nomad-pro-engine/tools/pdf_layout_check.py` and check the PDF has more than one page; add the `Outputs` row, then send both files to the user in chat only. If the PDF step errors (e.g. WeasyPrint missing), say so plainly; never send the dashboard instead.

**"For my accountant" (one step).** On "file for my accountant" or similar: current tax year by default (ask only in April–June, when last year is likelier); steps 2–3 without step 1's question; send both files with one line: "Travel and day log – 2026/27 (to 28 Sep 2026): PDF to read, CSV one row per date. 5 UK midnights · 5 UK work days unsure · 0 days not logged." + `Source: RFIG20710 · not tax advice`. Then offer a cover note they can paste and send themselves (never sent by you; core rules §9):
> Hi [name], attached is my travel and day log for 2026/27 to 28 Sep 2026 (PDF and CSV, one row per date), kept in Nomad Pro. It lists where I slept, UK work days and where the records sit; gaps are marked. It's my own record, not advice.

Open items (unsure work days, days not logged, no record pointer) show as counts in that line; after sending, offer once to clear them and re-export. Never hold the export back for them.

The engine's PDF: L6 in every footer; the counts (stage line only per core rules §3; **Work-day rule** as confirmed; day log with an **Evidence** column) plus timeline, stays, ties with pages, change log and counting method.

**Year-end:** from 6 April the check-in only catches up days to 5 April; the 7 April lockdown (`year-end-lockdown`, on by default) checks the year and makes the final export. Routine off and no export: the check-in makes it after the catch-up, with headline counts. Offer an updated export after late corrections; locked-year changes show `after_lock` in the change log. Calendar connected and reviews current: the lockdown first shows the pre-filled year (headline counts, stays, only unclear days: not logged, UK work `unsure`, conflicts, no record pointer); unanswered days stay gaps.

## Travel log Sheet: the single source
The Google Sheet **"Nomad Pro – Travel log"** holds all the user's data (days, records, trips, profile answers, changes, outputs); every output traces back to its rows. `daylog.json` is the engine's working copy, rebuilt from the Sheet. State: `profile.travel_log_sheet` `{mode: sheets | drive_copy | csv, spreadsheet_id, sheet_link, folder_id, last_read_at, last_written_at, offered_sheets_at}`.

**Tabs.** Row 1 a one-line note, row 2 headers, both frozen; no formulas; editable headers (bold) light blue, others grey. Notes: "Blue columns are yours to edit; I read them before every count." (`Profile`: "…I'll read changes back to you before using them."); `Summary`, `Changes`, `Outputs`: "Edits here are ignored."
* `Summary`: Item · Value · Source rows (Row IDs behind it) · As of. Rows: UK days this tax year ("5 of 16", `running_count.figures`) · Schengen last 180 ("77 of 90") · days not logged this tax year · each other stay limit in use ("Thailand (TH): 12 of 60") · Last updated.
* `Days` (A–K), one row per date from the first logged day to today, gaps included, oldest first: Row ID (`D-YYYY-MM-DD`) · Date · Tax year · **Country** · **Place** · **Also in (travel day)** · **UK work over 3h** · Records (Record IDs) · Confidence · Logged via · Locked.
* `Records`: Record ID (`R-0001`; a user-added row gets the next ID) · **Proves days** (Row IDs or a range `D-2026-09-14–D-2026-09-16`) · **Type** · **Description** · **File or link**.
* `Trips` (saved "what if" trips, rechecked weekly): Trip ID (`T-001`) · **Country** · **Place** · **First night** · **Last night** · **Status** · Result when saved ("UK 15 of 16") · Saved on · Last checked.
* `Changes`, every edit by the bot or user, newest last: Day changed (Row, Record or Trip ID) · Field · From · To · Via · Reason · Changed at.
* `Profile`: Section · Item · **Value** · Tax year · Agreed on · Source. One row per `onboarding` answer (prior years as "UK resident: Yes / No / Not sure" and "UK days: about N" or "More than 90: …"; left the UK; each passport, visa or permit with expiry; UK ties per tax year incl. Only home, Full-time UK work, Full-time work overseas as their claim; work-day rule versions in their words, Agreed on = `agreed_at`; check-in; calendar-review rhythm; backup delivery). Source: "You told me", "Setup", "Year-end review" or a Record ID. No addresses; UK places by label.
* `Outputs`, every export, PDF, dashboard, accountant file, records pack, backup and year-end file, newest last: Output ID (`O-0001`) · Date · Type · Date range · Rows used (`D-2026-04-06–D-2026-09-28`) · File location.

**Plain labels, mapped back when read.** Country "Portugal (PT)" (or "PT"/"Portugal"; else invalid) → `PT`. UK work "Yes"/"No"/"Not sure"/blank (blank = `n/a` on a non-UK day, `unsure` on a UK day). Confidence: "Has a record" `confirmed`, "You told me" `attested`, "Proposed, not confirmed" `inferred`, "Not logged" `unlogged`, "Planned" `planned`. Logged via: "Daily check-in" `checkin`, "Catch-up" `catchup`, "Setup" `onboarding`, "Calendar review" `weekly_review`, "Calendar" `calendar`, "Email" `email`, "Added in Sheet or import" `import`. Record type: plain names of schema `evidence.type` (Booking, Card payment, … Other). Trip status: "Idea (what if)" `idea`, "Booked" `booked`, "Taken" `confirmed_taken`, "Cancelled" `cancelled`. Changes Field/Via in plain words ("Place", "Your correction", "Record added", "Work-day rule fix", "Trip saved"). Locked "Yes" or blank.

**Build the engine copy from the Sheet (before every count, check-in, output and routine run).**
1. Read all tabs (`Days!A3:K`, `Records!A3:E`, `Trips!A3:I`, `Profile!A3:F`; `Changes`, `Outputs` for IDs); map labels back.
2. Compare with `~/nomad-pro-data/sheet/last-written/` (a CSV per tab from the last write): a changed editable cell is a user edit.
3. Candidate `daylog.json`: `days[]` from `Days`; `evidence[]` from the `Records` rows proving each day (`record_id`, `type`, `label` = Description, `url`/`file`/`pointer`); `planned_trips[]` from `Trips`; `profile` and `tie_answers` from `Profile`; from the current file only what the Sheet doesn't hold (calendar rules, `weekly_reviews`, `alerts_sent`, `rule_watch_log`, locks, `user_notes`, `user_limit`, private places). `validate`.
4. **The Sheet wins.** Valid edits go in with a `changes` entry `{at, field, from, to, via: "user_correction", reason: "edited in sheet"}`, also appended to `Changes`; a filled gap becomes `attested` (dated today), `logged_via: import`. Then replace `daylog.json`.
5. **Invalid rows** keep their last valid value and are flagged in one line ("2 Sheet rows I couldn't use: D-2026-09-12 country 'Portugall', D-2026-09-14 UK work 'maybe'. Fix them in the Sheet or tell me here."), until fixed. Never guess a fix.
6. **Profile edits** are read back before use, one per message ("You changed 2025/26 Family to No. Use that from now on? yes / no"; on yes save with `tie_answers_reviewed_at`); a work-day rule as in `onboarding` E (never re-tag earlier days without their OK); passports and expiry dates after `validate`. Until confirmed the old value stays and the row is flagged.
7. Locked years: core rules §8 and `year-end-lockdown` (their reason first, then append with `after_lock: true`). A cleared cell or deleted row is never a deletion: ask. A row changed in both Sheet and chat since the last write: keep both in `conflict` and ask. Edits to log-only columns, `Summary`, `Changes` or `Outputs` are ignored and mentioned once.
Mention what was taken in one line inside the next message ("Took 2 edits from your Sheet: D-2026-09-12, D-2026-09-14."), never a message of its own.

**Write to the Sheet.** Every change the bot makes (check-in, catch-up, review, record, trip, lock) goes to the Sheet first: update changed rows, append new ones and a `Changes` row; then rebuild `daylog.json`, run `summary`, rewrite `Summary`, and save each tab as CSV in `sheet/last-written/`. If a write fails, keep the change in `daylog.json` and the CSVs, say so in one line and write it next run.

**Outputs trace to rows.** Every PDF, CSV, dashboard, accountant file, records pack, backup and year-end file gets an `Outputs` row (date, type, date range, Row IDs used, file location) before it's sent, and the footer "Source: Nomad Pro – Travel log, rows <first Row ID>–<last Row ID>, generated <D Mon YYYY>": the CSV's last line; for the PDF render `--html-only`, add it under the L6 footer (L6 verbatim; a second line in `@bottom-center`), then WeasyPrint and `pdf_layout_check.py`; the dashboard takes it as `--source`. **Chat counts** come from the rebuilt copy and can name their source rows when asked ("UK days 5: D-2026-09-14, D-2026-09-22–D-2026-09-25"), matching `Summary`.

**Connections.**
* **Google Sheets (main path; packed, offered on day one; edit-access line in `onboarding` F).** Connector actions: create the spreadsheet once (seven tabs, notes, headers, shading, frozen rows), read range, update range, append rows. With Drive connected, move it into a "Nomad Pro" folder (`update_file` `parentId`; folder created once, `mimeType` `application/vnd.google-apps.folder`). Never a second Sheet: if the saved id is missing, search the title (Drive `search_files`) and ask first. Never share it or change its sharing.
* **Edit access.** Read-only or a failed write: one line ("I can read your Sheet but can't update it. Please reconnect Google Sheets with edit access."); work from the CSVs and catch the Sheet up once access works.
* **Not connected (they declined): CSVs, and one reminder.** The same seven tabs as CSVs in `~/nomad-pro-data/sheet/` (`Nomad Pro – Travel log – Days.csv` etc., same notes, IDs, labels) are the source; send them when asked. Remind them of Sheets once (`offered_sheets_at`). An edited CSV sent back goes through the same steps.
* **Drive-only fallback (they declined Sheets, connected Drive and want the log there).** Drive can create and export but not edit cells. Create once: the tabs as an `.xlsx` (`openpyxl`; `pip install openpyxl` if missing) via `create_file` (`base64Content`, `contentMimeType` `application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.spreadsheetml.sheet`, `parentId` the "Nomad Pro" folder, conversion on); read with `download_file_content` (`exportMimeType` the `.xlsx` type). Refresh only as a last resort, at most once a day: read edits first, upload a fresh copy, move the old one to "Nomad Pro/Older versions" with the date in its title (never trash or delete); the CSVs stay the source in between. Mention once that Sheets would keep one Sheet edited in place.

**Where's my data?** 2 lines: everything is in the Sheet "Nomad Pro – Travel log" (its link; the folder link on the Drive-only fallback; the CSVs here without Sheets), which they can check or edit any time; stored files are in `~/nomad-pro-data/evidence/` and `documents/`, linked from `Records`.
