# Onboarding (setup in chunks)

Not an interview: `nomad-pro-getting-started` decides **when**; this skill says **what** and where it's saved: the travel log Sheet (`export-travel-day-log`; answers in `Profile`), then `daylog.json` at the rebuild (schema `~/nomad-pro-engine/schema/daylog.schema.json`). One question per message; "not sure" and "skip" are fine. Never infer a status. Follow `nomad-pro-core-rules`. No destination questionnaire here.

**A. Previous 3 tax years:** for each, UK resident yes/no/unsure and roughly how many UK days (or "more than 90: yes/no/unsure"); Table A/B and 16/46 use the previous 3 (RDR3), the 90-day tie the previous 2 (RFIG20570). Save `profile.prior_years`, `tax_years[*].uk_resident_recorded`. Also the departure date, if they left (or plan to), with its tax year in short ("March 2026 falls in the 2025/26 tax year"); a possible split year is recorded as a claim only (RFIG21000).

**B. Basics:** what to call them; timezone (from where they sleep tonight; ask only if unclear). The check-in is already on (daily 21:00); change it only if they ask (time, weekly, "when I move", pause, stop). Save `profile.checkin` `{rhythm, time, timezone, weekly_line_day: "Sun"}`.

**C. Passports and documents:** which passport(s) they travel on (they drive stay limits; rules come from GOV.UK for British passports, so for others say so and point to their own government's travel advice), each expiry date, any visas or residence permits with expiry dates. Save `profile.passports`, `profile.documents_expiry` `{type, country, label, expires_on, added_at}` (`travel-rules-watch` reminds them 6 and 3 months ahead). Any nationality is fine; never stop because someone isn't British.

**D. UK ties, one at a time, citing each page:** family (RFIG20530: UK-resident spouse, civil partner or partner living together as if married; UK-resident under-18 children seen in the UK, 61-day figure); accommodation (RFIG20550, RFIG22170: each UK place as a label, relationship (own, rented, close relative, other relative, friend, hotel), available for a continuous 91 days?; 1 night vs 16 nights; create `accommodation_register` entries); work (RFIG20560: UK work over 3 hours a day, everyday meaning RFIG20740); 90-day (from A); country (RFIG20580: explained, the log computes it). Also: is a UK home their only home (RFIG20330); full-time UK work (RFIG20370); employment type and any claim of full-time work overseas (RFIG20140, recorded as their claim; ask for the contract via `evidence-and-documents`). Save `tax_years[YYYY/YY].tie_answers` + `tie_answers_reviewed_at`. Their answer is kept even if the log differs; flag, never overwrite. Unanswered ties keep the result line closed (core rules §3); say so once if they skip.

**E. Work-day rule: theirs, never a default.** One short question each, recorded exactly (work / non-work / ask me each time): job periods; UK weekdays; weekends; UK bank holidays; travel days into or out of the UK; travelling for work (ask each time / work day / follow travel-day setting) and the calendar words marking a work trip; exception keywords (e.g. `sick`, `less than 3 hours`) or none; how "more than 3 hours" is decided. Their own answer for any day always wins (tell them). Read the rule back in their words, ask "Is that right?", save only when confirmed: `profile.work_day_rules` entry `v1` (`effective_from`, `job_periods`, `no_job_periods`, `weekdays`, `weekends`, `public_holidays`, `travel_days`, `work_travel`, `work_travel_keywords`, `exceptions`, `over_3h_basis`, `user_answers_take_priority: true`, `agreed_at`, `agreed_via: "agreed with user in chat"`, `wording_shown`). Applying it to earlier days needs their OK (`applies_to_earlier_days_agreed: true`).

**F. Connections (packed, offered on day one; each can be declined; nothing is sent).** Google Calendar and Gmail (read-only), Google Sheets (edit access), Google Drive. All four are offered in message 2 (`nomad-pro-getting-started`, wording there), then whenever asked; Sheets gets one reminder in message 3. Declining changes nothing else: the check-in covers every day. Asking for Sheets, add: "I need edit access so I can keep your travel log Sheet up to date and read your own edits back." Once connected, create the Sheet once (seven tabs, CSV rows moved in, in the "Nomad Pro" Drive folder if Drive is connected; `export-travel-day-log`) and send its link in one line. Drive holds that folder (Sheet, backups, records) and, without Sheets, is the fallback. Read-only or a failed create/edit: say so in one line; ask them to reconnect with edit access. Save `profile.connections`, `profile.travel_log_sheet`.

Calendar connected (after message 3, or on connecting): agree calendar rules in one editable message: stays as an all-day event "Lisbon, PT"; an event per flight or train (or flight emails); work-exception words from their rule; UK stays labelled from the accommodation register; which calendars to read (default: main only); "Anything I can't place, I'll flag and ask about instead of guessing." Read back, save when confirmed: `profile.calendar_rules` `v1` (`effective_from`, `stay_title_format`, `travel_day_source`, `work_exception_words`, `uk_stay_labels`, `calendars_read`, `agreed_at`, `agreed_via`, `wording_shown`). Then ask the review rhythm (weekly Sunday 18:00 by default; fortnightly, monthly or quarterly) and switch on the calendar review at it, their timezone (packed one or create it; never a duplicate).

**G. Backups (on by default):** the monthly backup (1st, 09:00 their time) is on from the first count and delivers in chat. Ask about delivery right after message 3 if Drive is connected (or when they connect it or ask): "Your monthly backup comes here in chat. Save it to a Google Drive folder too (or instead)? If so, which folder?" Drive only if connected; never shared. Save `profile.backup` `{delivery, drive_folder, agreed_at}` (default `delivery: chat`).

**H. Record sources and status documents:** where records sit (email bookings, calendar, bank apps, maps timeline, photos); the Evidence column (link, stored file or pointer); offer to file status documents (contract, tenancy end, P85 acknowledgement) via `evidence-and-documents`.

**I. Trips and backfill:** upcoming trips → `planned_trips` + `travel-rules-watch`. Backfill before 6 April only for an earlier year they want ("Where were you sleeping in …?"): a record pointer or their dated statement (`attested`) per day; unknown days stay not logged; UK work via `srt_engine.apply_work_rule` (no rule: ask; unanswered stays `unsure`); travel days get both countries.

**J. Setup check** ("check my setup"; once after a connection is added): one line each, counts and links only: engine version (failing: `engine-setup`); travel log Sheet; Calendar (events, last 7 days); Gmail (booking emails since 6 April); Drive folder; routines on, with times (missing: create; a duplicate: ask); last backup. A failure: what it stops and the fix.

After a chunk that changes days or answers: `validate`, `summary`, report only what moved, as bars.

**How to use it** (once, after message 3): "Tell me where you are, or just tap yes at check-in. Ask 'what if I spend 10 days in London in December?' before you book. Say 'file for my accountant' for this year's log. Everything is in your travel log Sheet (or ask for the files); edit the blue columns any time and I'll pick up your changes. I also check HMRC's guidance and GOV.UK travel rules weekly and only message you if something changes. On the 1st of each month you get a backup of your records, and on 7 April I help you close the tax year." If the calendar is connected, add: "Each Sunday at 18:00 I go through your calendar with you and propose the nights for you to confirm." Close long setup summaries with L6.
