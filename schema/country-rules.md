## Verification notes (2026-09-26)

**Thailand.** GOV.UK Thailand entry requirements (updated 18 Sep 2026): *"From 15 September 2026, if you enter Thailand under the visa exemption scheme, you can stay for up to 30 days for tourism only"*; *"Visa-exempt entry for tourism is normally limited to two entries per calendar year"*; entrants before 15 Sep 2026 keep the period granted on arrival. Corroborated by the Thai Government Public Relations Department (4 Sep 2026, citing the MFA Department of Consular Affairs: 60-day scheme revoked, Royal Gazette 31 Aug 2026, effective 15 Sep 2026) and the MFA Department of Consular Affairs 30-day list dated 15 Sep 2026 (UK is no. 59). The previous 60-day exemption no longer applies to new entries.

**Cyprus.** GOV.UK Cyprus entry requirements (updated 14 Aug 2026): *"Cyprus is not part of the Schengen area"*; Cyprus and Schengen 90-day limits are counted separately. European Commission Schengen area page: the area has 29 countries; Cyprus *"participates in the Schengen cooperation. Nevertheless, internal borders controls have not yet been abolished by the Council."* The Commission's State of Schengen report COM(2026) 150 (18 May 2026) lists completing Cyprus' accession as a priority. No Council decision or accession date was found on 2026-09-26 (press, e.g. Cyprus Mail 4 Sep 2026, describes the decision as pending). Cyprus therefore stays on its own 90/180 row until an accession decision takes effect.

**Schengen membership.** 29 countries, Bulgaria and Romania from 1 Jan 2025 (European Commission).
