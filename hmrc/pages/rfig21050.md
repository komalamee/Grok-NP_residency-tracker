# Statutory Residence Test (SRT): Split year treatment: Case 1 - The relevant period

A relevant period is any period consisting of 1 or more days that:

- begins with a day that falls within the tax year
- is a day on which the individual does more than 3 hours work overseas
- ends with the last day of the tax year
