# Statutory Residence Test (SRT):International tax clarifications due to coronavirus (COVID:19):International tax clarifications due to coronavirus (COVID 19) - Work tie

An individual has a UK work tie if they work for more than 3 hours in the UK for at least 40 days in that tax year. (see [RFIG20560](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig20560))

The modification to the legislation will disregard any days the individual works for more than 3 hours in the UK if:

- the day falls in the period beginning with 1 March 2020 and ends with 1 June 2020
- on that day the individual is in the UK carrying out specified coronavirus disease related work, and
- the individual is resident in another country for tax purposes.
