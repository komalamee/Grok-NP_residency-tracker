# RDR3 Statutory Residence Test

Read the guidance to find out about the Statutory Residence Test (SRT) introduced in Finance Act 2013. The SRT allows you to work out your residence status for a tax year.

The guide explains how HMRC interprets the legislation to apply the SRT to your circumstances.

Read the guidance with the SRT legislation, which forms Schedule 45 to the Finance Act 2013 to gain a full understanding.
