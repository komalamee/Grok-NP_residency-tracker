# Statutory Residence Test (SRT):International tax clarifications due to coronavirus (COVID:19):International tax clarifications due to coronavirus (COVID 19) - Days of presence and tests affected

A day counts as a day spent in the UK if the individual is in the UK at the end of the day - midnight. There are 2 exceptions to this:

- if the individual was passing through the UK as a passenger (see [RFIG20730](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig20730))
- if the day was attributable to exceptional circumstances (see [RFIG22210](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22210) onwards)

The change to the legislation now allows for a third case if:

- the day falls in the period beginning with 1 March 2020 and ends with 1 June 2020
- on that day the individual is in the UK carrying out specified coronavirus disease related work, and
- is resident in another country for tax purposes

Tests affected

The following tests are affected by the modifications to the legislation, further details can be found by clicking on the links:

- the third automatic overseas test ([RFIG22450](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22450) and [RFIG22460](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22460))
- deemed days ([RFIG22470.](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22470))
- the second automatic UK test ([RFIG22480](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22480))
- the family tie ([RFIG22490](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22490))
- the accommodation tie ([RFIG22500](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22500))
- the work tie ([RFIG22510](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22510))
- the 90-day tie ([RFIG22520](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22520))
- the country tie ([RFIG22530](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22530))

Information on the type of records and how to inform HMRC can be found at [RFIG22550](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22550).
