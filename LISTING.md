## Long description

Built by British nomads, for British nomads.

If you've left the UK, HMRC's Statutory Residence Test turns on days, ties and work, and on the records behind them. Keeping those records day after day is the part nobody enjoys. Nomad Pro does it for you.

**The boring admin, managed for you.**
- **Check-ins in seconds.** Where you were, whether you worked, whether UK work ran over 3 hours, and where the record sits. Miss a week and it catches up every missed day in one go. It never fills a gap with a guess.
- **Your numbers, always current.** A dashboard and PDF show your UK days, UK work days and Schengen count against the HMRC figure for your recorded ties, with how many days of room you have left.
- **Calendar and Gmail, if you like.** Connect Google Calendar and it uses your calendar as a source of evidence, proposing the days for you to confirm, plus a short end-of-week review: "Here's what I think your week was. Is this right?" Connect Gmail and each day can carry a link to its booking or ticket email, or a screenshot you send. You get a clickable Evidence column in your log, dashboard and PDF.
- **Status documents filed.** Employment contract, termination notice, end of tenancy: filed against the tax years they cover.
- **Records pack in one request.** "Build a records pack for 2024/25 and 2025/26" gives you one zip with each year's Travel and day log (PDF and CSV), an index linking each day to its records, your files and documents, and a cover index. Ready for your adviser, and a PDF at the end of every tax year without asking.
- **HMRC's guidance in plain English.** Ask "I'm still on my UK GP register, how does that work?" and it searches HMRC's 143 gov.uk pages of Statutory Residence Test guidance. It quotes what HMRC says, with the page and date, or tells you plainly that the guidance doesn't cover it.
- **Leaving-the-UK checklist.** The official GOV.UK pages to look at when you go: P85, Self Assessment, National Insurance, State Pension, GP, council tax, student loan, overseas voting, driving licence and more. Each link is checked before it's sent, and there's a version for coming back.
- **Watches that stay quiet.** A weekly check of entry rules for everywhere you're heading and of HMRC's guidance. You only hear when something changes.

**Bonus: a concierge for your next move.** Tell Nomad Pro your budget and what matters to you, whether it's rent, food, safety, crime or getting around. It shortlists places that fit those preferences *and* your own log: your UK day count, the Schengen 90/180 window, each country's stay limit and GOV.UK entry rules. Every figure comes with its source and the date it was checked. Already have a plan? It models the trip before you book: "Lisbon 1 Feb–31 Mar would take your Schengen count to 88 of 90".

**What it doesn't do.** Nomad Pro keeps records and does arithmetic on them. It doesn't give tax, legal or immigration advice, and it doesn't decide anyone's residence. For that, take your records to a qualified adviser. Nomad Pro is not affiliated with HMRC.
